## The question, and where it stands

The question was whether a CYP or P-gp finding should read contraindicated ("Avoid together") without a label saying so. Since Ship 1.51 (7bdfa95), `pkSeverity()` stops at major ("Serious concern") for a strong inhibitor or inducer with a sensitive or narrow-therapeutic-index substrate, and "Avoid together" on these findings comes from a label pin. Kaleb approved pinning the 91 label-quoted pairs that had fallen to Serious concern, so those pairs read "Avoid together" again. This preview still caps every CYP/P-gp contraindicated finding that carries no label citation in `basis.ts` and reports what moves, as a cross-check. Where a named pair or class rule also says contraindicated, the pair keeps that verdict.

## Method and limits

- Catalog scan: every unordered pair, `analyze([a, b])` with no host and no entered amounts, the same as #66. Host-, phenotype-, and dose-driven findings are not counted.
- A finding is capped only if it is a CYP/P-gp perpetrator finding from `pkSeverity()` at contraindicated **and** `basisFor()` attaches no label citation (`fda-boxed`, `fda-pi`, `fda-warning`). Pair severity is recomputed as the highest remaining finding for the two-drug regimen.
- Gold-set cross-check uses `DEFAULT_HOST` and pair-level findings, matching `label-gold-set-eval.ts`.
- Counts reflect main's catalog and will shift after #58 and #60 merge. This is a cross-check, not a reviewed severity table.
