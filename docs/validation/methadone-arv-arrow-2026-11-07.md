## Why

The engine skipped the CYP3A4 inhibitor card ("higher methadone") and showed the `pd-methadone-ritonavir` card ("methadone may fall / withdrawal") only for `ritonavir` and `paxlovid` (`RITONAVIR_BOOST` in engine.ts). The saved methadone label says ritonavir-boosted protease inhibitors lower methadone.

## What the saved label says

Methadone HCl tablets SPL `540edd43-165d-4257-b989-3bccc8f54afb`, section 7 DRUG INTERACTIONS, as saved in `scripts/fixtures/label-drift-spl.json` (retrieved 2026-10-28). No live site was reachable from the build machine. Verbatim:

- Paradoxical Effects of Antiretroviral Agents on Methadone Hydrochloride Tablets:
  > Concurrent use of certain antiretroviral agents with CYP3A4 inhibitory activity, alone and in combination, such as abacavir, amprenavir, darunavir+ritonavir, efavirenz, nelfinavir, nevirapine, ritonavir, telaprevir, lopinavir+ritonavir, saquinavir+ritonavir, and tipranavir+ritonavir, has resulted in increased clearance or decreased plasma levels of methadone. This may result in reduced efficacy of methadone hydrochloride tablets and could precipitate a withdrawal syndrome. Monitor methadone-maintained patients receiving any of these anti-retroviral therapies closely for evidence of withdrawal effects and adjust the methadone dose accordingly.
- Highlights line in section 7: "Anti-Retroviral Agents: May result in decreased efficacy or, in certain cases, increased toxicity. (7)"
- Inhibitors of CYP3A4, CYP2B6, CYP2C19, CYP2C9, or CYP2D6 (same section): "Examples: Macrolide antibiotics (e.g., erythromycin), azole-antifungal agents (e.g. ketoconazole), protease inhibitors (e.g., ritonavir), fluconazole, fluvoxamine". The label lists ritonavir here as an example inhibitor that can raise methadone, and in the paragraph above as an antiretroviral that has lowered it. This branch follows the antiretroviral paragraph, which is specific to these drugs.

Other saved labels: Norvir, Kaletra, Prezista, Prezcobix, Reyataz, Evotaz and Paxlovid are in the fixture with section 4 (Contraindications) only, and none of those sections mentions methadone. Crixivan, Invirase, Aptivus, Viracept, Genvoya, Stribild, Symtuza and Tybost labels are not in the fixture. So the methadone label is the only saved source for the direction.

## What changed in the engine

- `src/lib/drugs/methadone-antiretroviral.ts` lists the rows above with their arrow and saved evidence. It replaces the `RITONAVIR_BOOST` check for methadone. `RITONAVIR_BOOST` is kept, unchanged, for the buprenorphine card only.
- For a listed row that carries a CYP3A4 inhibitor role, with methadone:
  - the CYP3A4 inhibitor card (`pk-CYP3A4-inhibitor-*`, "↑ exposure") is not emitted;
  - methadone is not a CYP3A4 victim on that row's CYP clock card, so the clock (and the CYP3A4 + P-gp dual card, which rides on the clock) no longer names methadone;
  - methadone is not a victim on the CYP3A4 phenoconversion card when every 3A4 inhibitor on the desk is a listed row;
  - `falls` rows show `pd-methadone-ritonavir`, major, "methadone may fall / withdrawal". Ritonavir and Paxlovid keep their card text from main. The other rows get text that quotes the label paragraph and names the regimen in the label's own words.
  - `unsettled` rows show a new `pd-methadone-arv-unsettled` card, moderate (Use care), "methadone direction not settled". Moderate because the label's highlights line says antiretroviral agents "May result in decreased efficacy or, in certain cases, increased toxicity." and the label asks for monitoring. It names no direction.
- Where the pair has no 3A4 inhibitor card any more, the existing competitive-substrate card (`pk-comp-CYP3A4`, minor) appears for rows that are themselves 3A4 substrates, the same as ritonavir on main.
- The window brief (OTP desk) shows the Paxlovid text only when ritonavir or Paxlovid is on the desk; other fall rows get a label-based withdrawal line, and unsettled rows get a "direction not settled" line. The independent-review line for `pd-methadone-ritonavir` now cites the label paragraph; `pd-methadone-arv-unsettled` has its own line.
- Not changed: buprenorphine cards; the standalone cobicistat (Tybost) row, its methadone card and its desk copy; every pair without methadone; every methadone pair with a row not listed above.
