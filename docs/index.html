Good to know

A few important questions.

Does “no mapped interaction” mean a combination is safe?

No. The checker can miss interactions and its map is not exhaustive. Review current prescribing information and use qualified clinical judgment.

Does FirstPass provide treatment or dosing advice?

No. It is an educational and clinical-review aid, not a dose calculator, personal treatment guide, or substitute for a clinician.

What should I do in an urgent situation?

Do not wait for an online checker. Contact local emergency services or a poison center for urgent illness, possible overdose, or immediate safety concerns.

!

Important limits

FirstPass is an educational and clinical-review aid. It is not FDA-cleared or FDA-approved, does not provide dosing instructions, and does not replace clinical judgment or current prescribing information. It can miss interactions; no finding does not mean a combination is safe. It is not intended to guide personal treatment decisions.
